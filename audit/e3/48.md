## Accuracy

Disagreements fixed:
1. `48-overview` pairs, "Why the drug makes sense". The hub said alosetron "slows the gut so effectively it can cause ischemic colitis". That causal link is not in the book and isn't established. The book says alosetron is a 5-HT₃ antagonist with life-threatening bowel complications, including ischemic colitis and constipation. Now reads: "slows the gut — and can cause severe constipation or ischemic colitis".
2. Screening Quick bullet said "FIT yearly"; the book's option is FOBT yearly. Changed to "FOBT/FIT yearly" (the text already said this).
3. The stool-blood test bullets dropped "depending on the type of test". This was a fidelity gap rather than a wrong fact. Bullets now lead with the test-type dependence.

Hub adds (correct, kept):
- Adhesions are the most common cause after surgery.
- "Feculent" as the term for foul orange-brown vomitus.
- IBS is functional, with no structural damage.
- Lynch genes are mismatch-repair genes.
- AP resection means a permanent colostomy.
- FIT listed with FOBT for yearly screening.
- Trauma is the "3rd leading cause of death **from injury**"; the book says only "third leading cause of death".
- Stoma has no pain nerves; Kehr sign via the phrenic nerve (in the `why` text).
- New in this pass:
  - An ascending colostomy pouch is worn at all times. The book doesn't state it outright, but its NCLEX 48.3 tests it.
  - The italic ch 45 cross-reference: guaiac test, no NSAIDs for 7 days, no red meat or vitamin C over 250 mg for 3 days; FIT needs no prep.

Book oddities kept or flagged:
- **Stool-test prep** differs between ch 48 (48 h: aspirin, vitamin C, iron, red meat) and ch 45 (gFOBT-specific schedule). Both kept, as instructed, and cross-referenced.
- **Cullen sign.** Box 48.7 locates it in the "flank area or umbilicus"; Turner is also flank. The hub keeps Cullen = umbilicus and Turner = flank, which is standard and consistent with part of the book's wording. "Flank" was not added to Cullen.
- **Over-85 rule.** The Legal/Ethical box says surgery is withheld when CRC is diagnosed after 85. This is a blanket statement, added as the book gives it.
- **Refined carbohydrates.** The book says they "decrease bowel transit time", which is arguably reversed. The hub's neutral "alter transit, favor polyps" was left unchanged.
- **Stoma color.** Healthy "dark red to pink" overlaps the warning "dark red, purplish, black". The hub mirrors the book.
- **Double-barrel colostomy.** Fig. 48.4 calls the transverse double-barrel colostomy common in emergencies, while the text calls double-barrel the least common. Both kept.
