The manual's IV title and actual IV-push actions disagree. The secondary-infusion procedure is a clearly labeled evidence-based supplement, not an invented school rubric. Faculty must confirm whether IV push, IVPB, addition to an IV bag, or a combination will be assessed, and which equipment will be used. No independent bag-admixture procedure is asserted as an assigned test-off.

Other syllabus lab topics (IV preparation/calculations, central/PICC care, ETT suction, chest tubes, cardiovascular/ECG, glucose/insulin, TPN/PPN, feeding tubes, ostomies) remain linked separately. They are not silently promoted from practice/lab topics to official test-offs. Their older summaries were not all re-audited in this change.

## Safety corrections and unresolved faculty questions

| Printed instruction / old summary | Reviewed guidance | Main reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air injection + epigastric auscultation to confirm tube position, p. 42 | Do not rely on an air whoosh or residual volume as proof. Use the actual tube's approved readiness/position checks; hold use for suspected displacement. An established gastrostomy is not treated as a newly inserted NG tube. | Open RN §15.2 |
| Blanket feed hold and fixed flush volumes, pp. 43–45 | Separate each medication. Use a verified formulation, medication-specific feed interruption, appropriate enteral connection/water, and individualized water plan including fluid restrictions/intake. | Open RN §15.2; NHS SPS |
| IV push-pause wording could stand in for administration time, pp. 52–54 | Verify drug-specific rate/compatibility/dilution; never force a flush. Clear drug-containing dead space at the safe medication delivery rate. | Open RN §2.5; CDC |
| Remain first five minutes of blood, p. 58 | Close observation during first 15 minutes of each component; baseline/15-minute/completion and ongoing observations per condition and facility policy. | Lifeblood monitoring; Open RN §3.4 |
| Reaction response incomplete as a general protocol | Stop immediately, assess/support ABCs, summon help, notify provider **and** transfusion service. Do not flush the blood-containing set into the patient. Preserve product/set and collect directed specimens. Severe reaction: do not restart component. | Lifeblood suspected reactions |
| Oral depth measurement, six-inch/cough target for trach, pp. 67–68 | Identify actual tube and approved shallow depth; insert without suction. Deep suction reserved for ineffective shallow suction. | AARC 2022 |
| Routine bag breaths and outdated AARC pressure attribution | Preoxygenate by the approved patient/device method. Adult AARC pressure magnitude limit is below 200 mm Hg, not a target; manual 80–120 is presented as the lab's printed setting pending policy. Adult catheter should occlude less than half the airway lumen. Suction no longer than 15 seconds; reassess/recover before repeat. No routine airway saline instillation. | AARC 2022 |
| Peroxide/brush universal cannula procedure, pp. 69–70 | Device-specific reusable cleaning; disposable cannula replaced, not cleaned for reuse. Maintain outer-tube stability/security and prescribed respiratory support. | Open RN §22.6; NTSP; Medtronic |
| Two validated practices and request after third practice, p. 5 | Both statements are shown; faculty must reconcile the requirement. Entered dates cannot establish permission to test. | Manual p. 5 |
| “Critical Skill Items” headings interpreted as specific auto-fail rows | Safety checkpoints are study priorities. Obtain the current evaluator rubric; do not manufacture automatic failures. | Syllabus p. 5; manual headings |

The seven guides and relevant old manual summaries are aligned on these corrections. The old blanket advice to perform the unsafe row for evaluation has been removed from the targeted stations. Two other old flags (IM aspiration and catheter-balloon pretesting) now direct faculty/manufacturer clarification instead of blanket deference to a conflicting checklist. This does not claim a new full clinical review of those additional procedures.

## State and scoring

- New state: `clin-skill-trainer-v1`. Included by the existing `clin-` progress export/import prefix. Existing `clin-lab-v1` records stay separate.
- One stable ID per question and checkpoint. Questions/options shuffled; short rounds balance selected stations; full rounds include all six questions per selected station.
- Single-choice exact answer; select-all exact set; sequence exact checkpoint order. This is a practice score, not an assertion of real NCLEX scoring or physical proficiency.
- Sequence scenarios explicitly exclude already completed steps. The reaction sequence is a dependent specimen-collection workflow; it does not impose a unique order on parallel stabilization tasks.
- Every wrong answer updates cumulative misses immediately, even if the learner leaves before finishing. A correct retry resolves but retains the item; include corrected misses to replay it; a later wrong answer reopens it. Selected stations filter all stored attempts, not just the latest round.
- Current choices, shuffled presentation, feedback/position and completed summary restore on reload. Duplicate submissions do not count twice. Invalid session data is discarded without losing valid miss/rehearsal records. History is bounded at 50 completed rounds; cumulative misses are separate.
- Rehearsal shows title cues first; action/rationale is absent from the answer area until comparison. Notes and independent/prompted/missed ratings persist. These ratings do not modify faculty dates.
