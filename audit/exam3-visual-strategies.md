# Full Exam 3 visual study and strategy rollout

All 29 existing lessons in the 17 assigned Exam 3 topic groups now have a topic-specific visual explanation, coached activity and worked question approach. Chapters 45, 46, 47, 53, 55 and 56 retain the assigned scope represented by the existing lessons. Full source notes remain linked; this is not a new claim that the 29 core lessons replace every assigned chapter detail.

## Content and interaction

- 29 visuals: mechanism/decision cards, comparisons, a patient-oriented quadrant diagram and a timed-collection timeline.
- 90 coached decisions: three per lesson for the 28 newly expanded lessons, plus the preserved six-decision hypoglycemia pilot. The collection activity includes a keyboard-operable ordering task. Quadrants can be selected from the patient's perspective.
- 29 worked topic strategies separate the task, relevant cue, reasoning and distractor trap. Coached activities offer optional hints. Independent checkpoints hide the explanation and hints until submission.
- All 58 pre-existing graded question variants, including their stems, options, keys and rationales, are unchanged. Lesson IDs, progress keys, scheduling and clinical-judgment scoring stay compatible.
- Activities persist with lesson drafts through reloads and lesson switching. Coached completion does not award mastery or review credit. Reopening explanations makes a session early practice rather than a delayed recall review.
- Notes for linked assigned topics have in-place visual/strategy reviews. Checked Mastery items show question-format guidance and up to three matched topic reviews. No strategy panel is shown before an answer is checked.
- A learner can identify a review focus: content, reading the task, prioritization or format. These are optional self-assessments, not automatically inferred diagnoses. Lesson reflections save with progress; Mastery reflections save to the item log and completed report without changing scores.
- The strategy reference indexes all 29 lessons. Audio remains optional. Verified word-part entries are reused; unknown term splits are not guessed.

## Strategy corrections

The shared strategy descriptions no longer teach that absolute words are automatically wrong, similar options must both be rejected, the broadest option generally wins, or an actual problem always outranks a serious imminent risk. Assessment depends on whether essential data are missing and whether immediate support is needed. The reference corrects the blanket claim that guessing has no penalty on plus/minus items, distinguishes local checkpoint scoring, and makes delegation dependent on jurisdiction, policy, competence and circumstances.

Primary examination references: [NCSBN 2026 test plans](https://www.nclex.com/test-plans.page), [official item-format/scoring FAQ](https://www.nclex.com/faqs.page), [official preparation](https://www.nclex.com/prepare.page) and [delegation guidance](https://www.ncsbn.org/delegation).

## Clinical review and provenance

Original activities were written from each existing lesson's assigned-book locator and source-linked notes, with targeted primary-source verification. The raw textbook is not republished. Source metadata is compiled with each visual from `studio-sources.js`.

Safety checks include the 2024 adult DKA/HHS consensus potassium threshold (<3.5 mmol/L: replace and delay insulin until >3.5), continued ketone/acidosis evaluation after glucose improves, the ACG 2024 H. pylori test-of-cure intervals, ADA 2026 diagnostic/monitoring and hospital principles, type 1 basal coverage during fasting, device-specific CGM confirmation, and treatment-specific medication warnings. Other primary references include NIDDK, NCI, ATA, MedlinePlus, OpenStax and official DailyMed prescribing information. No new individualized drug dose is prescribed. The original full notes are not newly certified in their entirety.

## Validation

`tools/check_e3_studios.js` covers all 29 metadata sets, 90 activity paths, every wrong-answer recovery path, duplicate submissions, ordering, saved-state recovery, independent scoring, topic/case matching and 58 unchanged graded variants. Existing lesson/scheduling, source-link/study-guide, question-format, clinical-judgment, missed-question and chapter-filter checks remain applicable.

Browser QA exercises all 29 lessons on desktop and phone: coached activities, intentional wrong answers, reloads, optional hints, recall with explanations hidden, independent scoring and reflections. Desktop checks both graded variants for every lesson. It also checks the linked source-note panels, strategy index, due-review protection and an actual checked Mastery item's reflection through reload and report completion. Small-screen layouts and screenshots are inspected separately.
