## Accuracy

**Disagreement fixed**
- Hub said gastric-ulcer pain is "not relieved by food" (47-overview pairs) and "food doesn't help" (47-peptic why/whyShort). Book Table 47.2 says pain is *worsened* by eating, and night pain is rare. I changed both to "worsened by food / food makes it worse", added "rarely at night", and put the full table in the PUD topic. This was imprecise rather than wrong.

**Checked and matching the book.** Antacid timing (Mg-Al 2 h, Al 1 h after meals and at bedtime; 1–2 h from other drugs); sucralfate (1 h before and 2 h after meals and at bedtime; 30 min); PPI 30 min before the main meal (rabeprazole after the morning meal); IV pantoprazole (filter, own line); tetracycline 1 h/2 h; metronidazole no alcohol for 3 days after; triple therapy 10–14 days; pain clock (30–60 min vs 1½–3 h and 1–2 a.m.); ages 50–70/20–50; FFP at PT > 1.5×; < 500 mL vs > 1 L/24 h; NPO 4–6 h (this chapter; the 6–8 h in chapter 45 is also the book); NG clamp at ~72 h with residual ≤ 50 mL/30 min; EGD every 4–6 weeks; dumping at 20 min vs 1–3 h; octreotide 30 min before meals; delayed emptying ~1 week; incision checks every 8–12 h; 5-year survival 30%/70%; average age 68; risk 2–3×.

**Hub adds (correct general knowledge, kept).** Late dumping named "hypoglycemia" (the book describes the insulin excess and its symptoms without the word); "avoid concentrated sweets"; Al and Mg antacids offset each other's bowel effects; 10–14 days applied to quadruple therapy too (the book gives the duration for triple only); IV PPI for stress-ulcer prevention (practice item; the book names IV famotidine); BUN rising from digested blood (ADPIE why, 47|c|11); mucus, bicarbonate and blood flow as parts of the barrier; lifelong B₁₂ injections after total gastrectomy (47|c|6; the book says "as prescribed"); my psychosocial examples (housing, money, depression), taken from the book's NGN case; K⁺ as the electrolyte to replace (the book's NGN uses NS + 20 mEq K).

**Book oddities kept**
1. The chapter opening says acute-gastritis mucosal changes "typically heal after several months", but pathophysiology and treatment twice say recovery takes "a few days". The hub keeps "a few days".
2. The urea breath test is described with "radioactive carbon (¹³C urea)". ¹³C is a stable isotope (¹⁴C is the radioactive one). The hub says "labeled carbon", which doesn't conflict, but an exam item may use the book's word.
3. The book blames all post-gastrectomy iron, folate, calcium and vitamin D deficits on "reduction of intrinsic factor", which really explains only B₁₂. The hub mirrors the book (it adds fast transit); kept.
4. Rabeprazole is a "sustained-release capsule" in Table 47.1 but an "enteric-coated tablet" in the text. The hub's "don't crush" is true either way.
5. "Achieving homeostasis" during hemorrhage should read hemostasis; the hub uses hemostasis.
6. The chapter never mentions NG lavage, and neither does the hub, so there is nothing to reconcile.

**Outside my files (not edited; need a decision)**
- Existing NGN 47|n|3 ("H. pylori therapy teaching"): the stem says *triple* therapy, yet a keyed-correct option is about bismuth, which belongs to quadruple therapy in the book. Suggest rewording the stem to "triple or quadruple therapy". I can't edit existing items.
- `data/e3/drugs-gi.js` sucralfate card says to separate other drugs by **2 h**. Table 47.1 says not within **30 min** of antacids *or other drugs*.
