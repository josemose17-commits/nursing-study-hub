Grade calculator

Type each score you have (as a percent). It shows your grade so far and what you need on everything left. Weights are from the syllabus: 5 exams at 14% each, the final 15%, Sherpath and quizzes 10%, the two mental health papers 2.5% each.

The syllabus says both “a minimum theory grade of 75%” and “a final grade of 78% must be achieved” — the calculator shows both. Ask your instructor which one decides passing.
