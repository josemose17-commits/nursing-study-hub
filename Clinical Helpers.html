Lab ranges for the Clinical Reasoning Plan of Care

Same rows as the plan’s lab tables. Adult ranges; labs differ slightly, so write the range printed on your patient’s report in the “Normal Range” column. Values marked ★ are the ones your med-surg book gives.

Erikson’s stages

The plan of care asks which stage your patient is in and whether they have reached it. Behaviors on the right show the stage was met; the opposite shows it wasn’t.

SBAR practice

The plan of care asks for two SBARs — one to update the provider, one to hand off to the next nurse. Practice here; it saves on this device only. Use initials only — never a real patient’s name or details that identify them.

Process recording: check a line

Type one thing you said to your client, exactly as you said it. The checker looks for well-known therapeutic and non-therapeutic patterns. It can’t hear tone or see the whole conversation — use it as a second look, then write the analysis yourself with your textbook.

The template (columns)

Student verbatim · Client verbatim · Therapeutic technique · Non-therapeutic technique · Your analysis / why

From the guidelines

  • Pick an appropriate time and place; introduce yourself.
  • Record a 5-minute segment. Keep it simple — the weather, the news, a holiday, a book, favorite foods. Stay away from religion and politics.
  • Note your body language, the client’s, and how engaged the client was.
  • Write it down verbatim right after you leave the room.
  • For every non-therapeutic reply, write what you could have said instead to invite the client’s feelings.
