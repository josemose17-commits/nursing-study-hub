Before a test-off (from the manual)
  • At least two satisfactory practices, each dated and initialed by your faculty or clinical instructor. The manual also says to request the test-off after your third practice.
  • Sign up for practice and the test-off through the skills-lab schedule, never during another class. Some test-offs need a partner, but you sign up and are graded on your own.
  • Bring your checklist and the right ATI test-off forms, your stethoscope and penlight, and your drug book for IV and medication test-offs. Without them, the test-off can be rescheduled.
  • Can’t make it? Tell the skills lab or your clinical instructor as soon as you can. An unsatisfactory test-off means remediation, then reschedule right away.
  • In the lab: uniform or lab jacket, student ID, hair tied back, no artificial nails or extra jewelry, no food or drink, phones off. Wear gloves to handle manikins, and use soap, not Betadine, as lubricant on them.
