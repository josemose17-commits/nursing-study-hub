NRS 114 · Med-Surg 1 · Exam 3

Food in, hormones in balance.

Chapters 45–47 (GI assessment, the mouth and esophagus, the stomach) and 53, 55 and 56 (the endocrine glands, the thyroid and parathyroids, and diabetes). The notes come from your med-surg book; the Why it works cards use your A&P, chemistry, microbiology, pharmacology, psychology and pathophysiology books to explain them. Everything follows your professor’s study guide.

Ready now: a day-by-day study route to October 28, the map (all six chapters, plus 48–52 for the final, organ → molecule breakdowns, Why it works cards from your other books, pictures and videos, every word defined, a reference strip of the numbers they use), the drug cards, the Mastery test (every NGN question type, scored the way the NCLEX scores it, plus unfolding case studies), skills checklists and the NCLEX strategies page. The final has its own section (the Final switch above). The Exam 3 test follows the study guide: 60 questions — 20 on chapter 56, 10 each on 55, 46 and 47, and 5 each on 53 and 45 — and only the items it names in 53, 45 and 46.

NRS 114 · Med-Surg 1 · Final

Everything, by topic.

The comprehensive final is 15% of the grade, and its topics come from your med-surg book: professional nursing and clinical judgment; older adults, rehabilitation and end of life; emergencies, environmental emergencies and disasters; fluids, electrolytes, acid–base and IV therapy; inflammation, immunity and allergy; and the four respiratory chapters. On top of those come the GI and endocrine chapters from Exam 3 and chapters 48–52.

Ready now: the Final map and the Final Mastery test with five topic chapters — fluid and electrolyte balance (Ch 13), acid–base balance (Ch 14), IV therapy (Ch 15), inflammation and immunity (Ch 16), and allergy and immunity conditions such as anaphylaxis, lupus, HIV, Lyme disease and transplant rejection (Ch 17): notes with a Quick version, every word defined, and NGN, NCLEX, recall and myth questions — plus the GI and endocrine chapters and 48–52. The other topic chapters are being added a few at a time; next up are the breathing chapters (Ch 22–25).
