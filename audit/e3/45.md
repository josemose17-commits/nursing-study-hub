## Accuracy

**Disagreements fixed**
- **ALT/AST.** The hub said levels are "highest with necrosis (hepatitis much more than cirrhosis)". The book's text puts the highest levels in necrosis "such as hepatitis and cirrhosis", and Table 45.2 has ALT rising significantly in hepatitis, moderately in cirrhosis and mildly in pancreatitis. I rewrote the line to the book's grading.
- **C. difficile.** The hub silently used the current name, *Clostridioides difficile*; the book says *Clostridium difficile*. The notes now give the book's name with "(now named *Clostridioides*)".
- **Incomplete Quick bullets.** These were not errors in the text, but the Quick bullets for potassium, calcium and bilirubin dropped facts the text gave (critical values, sodium loss, GGT). They now keep every fact.

**Hub adds (correct, kept)**
- CEA and CA 19-9 are "not to diagnose".
- Guaiac detects peroxidase activity; FIT uses antibodies to human hemoglobin.
- "Free air under the diaphragm."
- Dullness over fluid.
- Ulcerative colitis listed as IBD.
- Kupffer cells take up "old" RBCs (the book says "anemic").
- Intake and output during prep.
- Pregnancy screen before contrast (ADPIE).
- In the q file: prep is finished when returns are clear or pale yellow; clay-colored stool; smoking raises CEA; reversal agents; no sedation for the capsule.

**Book oddities kept (exam follows the book)**
- RR < 10 or exhaled CO₂ < 20% → sternal rub. Already settled; capnography is normally read in mmHg.
- gFOBT: no NSAIDs for 7 days; no red meat or vitamin C > 250 mg for 3 days. Chapter 48 gives 48 h.
- EGD NPO 6–8 h.
- Table 45.2 gives ammonia as 10–80 **mg/dL**; the usual unit is mcg/dL. The hub shows the range without a unit.
- Serum amylase: the text says it stays up to 5 days, but the urine section says serum is normal within 1–2 days. The hub keeps "about 5 days", with urine amylase at 5–7 days.
- The provider's sequence lists percussion after deep palpation. The hub follows the book.
- "Less specific than a colonoscopy" for the stool DNA kit (sensitivity is meant). Kept.
- Colonoscopy vital signs: "q15 min until stable" in the text vs "q15–30 min until alert" in Box 45.2. The hub uses the box.
- Stool kits: "multisample" vs "one stool sample". The hub gives no count.
